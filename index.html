    Questions

    What buyers ask us.

    Can I buy on Mallorca if I don't live in Spain?

    Yes. You need a Spanish tax number (NIE) and a Spanish bank account, which your lawyer can arrange, often before you arrive.

    What does buying cost on top of the price?

    Plan on roughly 10 to 15 percent of the price for transfer tax (or VAT on a new build), the notary, the land registry and legal fees. We give you a written estimate for every home.

    Do you charge buyers a fee?

    No. Our fee is paid by the seller. If you hire us to search for you, we agree a search fee up front.

    Can I view a home without flying in?

    Yes. Every home has a 3D model and a film, and an adviser can walk you through it on a live video call.

    How long does a purchase take?

    Usually six to ten weeks from the reservation to the notary, depending on the checks and on how you are paying.
